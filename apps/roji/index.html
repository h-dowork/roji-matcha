Our Story

Rooted in the
ancient path

Roji was born from a single cup of matcha shared in a quiet garden in Uji. We set out to bridge generations of Japanese tea craftsmanship with the modern world — without shortcuts.

  • Shade-Grown 30 Days
    Boosts L-theanine and chlorophyll for a naturally sweet, umami-rich cup
  • Stone Ground Granite
    Slow millstones preserve delicate amino acids destroyed by modern rollers
  • Direct Farm Sourcing
    We partner directly with Uji's Nishi family — no brokers, no compromises

From Leaf to Cup

Four steps, zero compromise

  1. 01

    Cultivation

    Tencha leaves grown under bamboo shade for 30 days before the first harvest of spring.

  2. 02

    Steaming & Drying

    Fresh leaves are steamed within hours of harvest, then air-dried to lock in colour and nutrients.

  3. 03

    Stone Grinding

    Slow granite millstones — one rotation per minute — produce the silkiest, brightest powder.

  4. 04

    Sealed & Shipped

    Nitrogen-flushed, light-sealed tins shipped direct from Uji — peak freshness guaranteed.

Our Philosophy

Slow down.
Taste deeply.

In a world optimised for speed, matcha asks something different of you. To measure, to whisk, to pause. Roji exists to honour that pause — the few quiet minutes that belong entirely to you.

  • Provenance Every tin traceable to its exact field and harvest date
  • Simplicity Nothing added. Nothing removed. Just tencha leaf, ground fine
  • Intentionality Designed to slow you down, not speed you up
  • Sustainability Carbon-offset shipping, recyclable tins, fair farm wages
